The **Pedagogy** index gathers the teaching notes. A pedagogy note differs from a
[reference](reference.html) note in its *contract with the reader*: reference
records how a thing works for someone who already has the map; pedagogy **builds
the map** — ground-up, plain words first, jargon glossed at first use, diagrams
that carry meaning, and it earns its keep by ending at a **real decision or bug**
the reader can now judge for themselves.

House rules for writing one: teach one slice, not the world; every concept
arrives with the problem that makes it necessary; prefer a true story (a bug, a
fork, a review fight) as the finale — teaching without a point is a tour.
