Every piece of work the [Conductor](/conductor) runs has a card or a row. Shut, it says where the work stands, where its time went and what each of its terminals is doing, and carries any question waiting for you. Open, it stays short, with the full history one click away.

## Where the cards are

The works take the wide side of the Conductor, and the chat is a panel on the right. While a file is open, [the reader](/read-a-plan) takes the works' place, and **← Work** brings them back as they were. The works scroll by themselves.

On a phone the works are the **Work** tab, beside **Chat**. The tab shows how many works need you. The status line's **N pieces of work** opens it, and so does tapping a work chip in the chat.

The works come in four groups:

| Group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs you · N** | Works waiting on you, as cards with their question: one asked a question, another holds a green PR handed over for you to merge. |
| **In progress · N** | Every other work that is building something, one row each. |
| **Watching · N** | Terminals you asked the Conductor to watch, one row each. |
| **Finished** | Works that ended, collapsed until you click it, one line each: title and repository, how it ended, how long it ran, and when. |

A row puts the title, the stage line, the terminals and the age side by side. Below 1280px wide its terminals drop under its title. On a phone every work in progress is a card. With nothing in progress the list says **Nothing in progress.**

## A work's face

| Line | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Title and age | What the work is, and how long it has run. |
| **repo · host · mode** | Where it is, then the PR as a link: `PR #n`, or `draft PR #n` for a draft. While in progress, **you 12m** is its total wait on you, from the first whole second. |
| Where it stands | Who it waits on and why, coloured by them, and for how long. Under **Needs you** it leads with what is wanted, as in **Needs you · Merge PR #700 · green for 12m**. |
| Stage strip | Its six stages, below. |
| Terminals | One row each: its role, its name (click it to go to the tile), what it runs, how it stands and for how long, **Look** (a [live window](/tiles#look-from-the-conductor) on its screen), and **Take over** or **Hand back**. |
| The question waiting for you | Its form, to answer without picking the work. The same decision the chat and the reader show: answering it in any of them answers it. |

A PR recorded before kolu kept PR addresses shows its number as plain text. A work still in progress gets the link the next time kolu reads its PR.

## The stage strip

The strip is one thin line of six segments, always in this order: **Plan**, **Approve**, **Build**, **Review**, **Checks**, **Merge**. Each segment is coloured by whose time that stage mostly was: violet you, amber CI, teal the agents. Every segment is named under its bar, and every stage the work has been in prints its own duration there too, such as **Plan 4m**, **Build 3m** or **Checks 1h 2m**, with no hover needed; the stage the work is in is the one whose time keeps growing. A stage not reached yet has its label and no time. The label is the emphasised one for the stage the work is in. Hover a segment for its exact time and whose time it was.

Which stages a work has comes from its mode:

| Mode | Its stages |
| --- | --- |
| Full | All six. |
| Trivial | **Build**, **Checks** and **Merge**. Nobody plans, approves or reviews, so those segments read as not happening. |
| Watch | None, and no strip. |

- The stage the work is in is marked with a ring.
- A dashed segment is a stage that did not happen.
- A faint segment is a stage not reached yet.
- In a full work, answering the setup form counts as Plan time, since there is no plan to approve yet. A trivial work has no Plan, so its setup, and the Conductor starting its implementor, are in no stage. That time still counts in **you 12m** when it was yours.
- The build ends when the implementor's PR is ready for review: at once for a PR opened ready, and when a draft is marked ready. A PR turned back into a draft returns the work to Build. A PR that was already there when the implementor joined, such as one a bot opened, does not end it by being open. If it is a draft, marking it ready does.
- CI's time is **Checks**, except on a draft, where it is still **Build**. A green PR handed to you is always **Merge**.

A work whose mode is not chosen yet shows six quiet segments.

On a card, the strip is the index into the timeline. Clicking a segment that has happened opens the card on that stage's events.

## What every card offers

Under a card's stage strip, and at the top of an open row:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open PR #N ↗** | Opens the pull request on its forge. It is there while a green PR is waiting for you to merge it. There is no merge button: you merge on the forge, or tell the Conductor to. |

Picking a work points the chat at it, so the card no longer needs an **About this work** button of its own. A work chip in the conversation still offers one, because there is no card to pick there.

## Reach a work from anywhere

Every work has an address, `#/work/…`. Opening it enters the Conductor, picks that work and scrolls to it, or to one question of it. A link to a work kolu no longer holds says so. [Link to any view](/power-features#link-to-any-view) lists the address forms.

The command palette lists what needs you first: a **Needs you** row says what is wanted and then names its work, and a **Works** row gives the title and where the work stands. Both keep their own headers while you search. The **◆** mark on a [dock row](/dock#a-row-in-a-piece-of-work) is a link to that terminal's work.

## The picked work

Click a work, a card or a row, and it is picked: it fills the works' side, and the other works fold to a rail of one line each, a strip of initials below 1280px wide. The chat narrows to the picked work. Click **← All work**, or the work's head again, to go back.

A work that needs you and a finished work can be picked too. On a phone the picked work is a page of its own, with **← All work** above it. A reload stays on the picked work, and the work's address opens it picked.

A switch in the picked work's head has two views:

- **Terminals**: each terminal of the work live, in a pane with **Take over** or **Hand back** and **Open tile**, as [Look](/tiles#look-from-the-conductor) shows it. One or two terminals stack. Three or more, or a window under 1280px wide, show one pane with a tab per terminal. On a phone, the key bar is under the pane once the terminal is yours.
- **Details**: the terminals' rows and the parts below. Clicking a stage on the strip shows Details.

Terminals is in front while a terminal of the work is up, Details otherwise. The details hold:

| Part | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Files | The worktree's `.waterfall/` files, at any depth, and the copies kolu kept. A folder is one chip with its file count, such as `mockup/ · 49`. Click it to unfold it. Click a file to open it in [the reader](/read-a-plan). |
| Pull request | Its number as a link: open, merged or closed, and **draft** while it is one. The checks or the last result, failed ones in one line (`7 failing: ci::nix, ci::unit, +5`) to click. While open, whether GitHub would merge it. |
| Setup | One line: the mode, each role's harness and model, and what happens on green. |
| Decisions | The latest three, one line each. **all N** shows the rest. Click a row for the question. |
| Timeline | The events of one stage, with their time and duration. |

The timeline shows the stage the work is in, or the one you clicked on the strip, and a finished work's last stage. **whole timeline · N events** unfolds all of it in place. A watch, which has no stages, shows its latest five events.

A question reads **Asked:** and your answer **You:**. A run of files one agent wrote into a folder is one row, such as **Wrote mockup/ · 49 files**. A line too long for the row is cut, and a folder's row lists its files, with **more** beside it.

### What a timeline keeps

A timeline is kept short in four ways:

- A work keeps its latest **30** agent state changes; older ones fold into one **N earlier agent state changes** line.
- While a PR's checks run, only GitHub's latest answer about merging it is kept.
- One commit keeps at most **8** check results and **8** merge answers. Past that, an old one in the middle is dropped, never the first or the newest.
- kolu lists the first **200** files and folders in `.waterfall/`, and draws a run of files written into one folder as one row.

Nothing else is dropped, and kolu keeps the **last 20** finished works with their files.

## How a work ends

A work ends three ways: the forge merges or closes its pull request, you click **Stop** on the card and confirm, or the Conductor stops it. Declining the setup of a work nothing has happened in removes it.

An ended card's line says how it ended, such as **Merged #181 · 1d ago**. Open, it shows where the time went, the kept files, the decisions and the timeline, where the merge and the end it caused are one row. It never shows terminals, a lock or a question, and it offers **Reopen**.

kolu copies the work's `.waterfall/` files when it ends, up to 50 MiB per work. Under **Files (kept)**, a line starting **Not kept:** names what was left out and why: a file past that size, a listing that stopped at 200 files and folders, or a copy that failed.
