Every piece of work the [Conductor](/conductor) runs has a card or a row. Shut, it says where the work stands, where its time went and what each of its terminals is doing, and carries any question waiting for you. Open, it stays short, with the full history one click away.

## Where the cards are

The works take the wide side of the Conductor, and the chat is a panel on the right. While a file is open, [the reader](/read-a-plan) takes the works' place, and **← Work** brings them back as they were. The works scroll by themselves.

On a phone the works are the **Work** tab, beside **Chat**. The tab shows how many works need you. The status line's **N pieces of work** opens it, and so does tapping a work chip in the chat.

The works come in four groups:

| Group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs you · N** | Works waiting on you, as cards with their question: one asked a question, another holds a green PR handed over for you to merge. |
| **In progress · N** | Every other work that is building something, one row each. |
| **Watching · N** | Terminals you asked the Conductor to watch, one row each. |
| **Finished** | Works that ended, collapsed until you click it, one line each: title and repository, how it ended, how long it ran, and when. |

A row puts the title, the stage map, the terminals and the age side by side. When the works column is narrower than about 830px, its terminals drop under its title. On a phone every work in progress is a card. With nothing in progress the list says **Nothing in progress.**

## The works sidebar

A sidebar beside the works lists every work, one entry each, whether a work is picked or not. It stays in place and scrolls by itself. A phone has no sidebar.

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Top row | **All work** names where you are. With a work picked it is **← All work**, which goes back. The row keeps one height, so the entries never move. |
| **Needs you · N** | Pinned at the top, each work with the violet chip saying how long it has waited. |
| Groups | **In progress**, **Watching** and **Finished**, each counting its works. **Finished** folds with the list's own **Finished**. |
| An entry's first line | The repository's monogram, the title, and how long the work has stood where it is. |
| An entry's second line | The [dock](/dock) status mark of each terminal (three, then **+N**), the dock's PR mark, and where the work stands. |

Drag the sidebar's edge to make it wider or narrower, as you do the Dock's. Double-click the edge to reset it. kolu remembers the width on this device.

In a window under 1280px wide the sidebar is a strip of initials. A work that needs you wears a violet ring, and hovering shows its title, where it stands and for how long.

## A work's face

| Line | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Title and age | What the work is, and how long it has run. |
| **repo · branch · host · mode** | Where it is: the repository's monogram, the branch in the dock's colour, then the PR as a link (`PR #n`, `draft PR #n`) and, while live, the dock's PR mark. In progress, **you 12m** is its total wait on you. |
| Where it stands | Who it waits on and why, coloured by them, and for how long. Under **Needs you** it leads with what is wanted, as in **Needs you · Merge PR #700 · green for 12m**. |
| Stage map | Its six stages and the loops it has gone round, below. |
| Terminals | One row each: its [dock](/dock) status mark, its role, its name (click it to go to the tile), what it runs, how it stands and for how long, **Look** (a [live window](/tiles#look-from-the-conductor)), and **Take over** or **Hand back**. |
| The question waiting for you | Its form, to answer without picking the work. The same decision the chat and the reader show: answering it in any of them answers it. |

A PR recorded before kolu kept PR addresses shows its number as plain text. A work still in progress gets the link the next time kolu reads its PR.

A loop the work went round, **Review** and **Build** or **Build** and **Checks**, says how often (**×6**). Across a doubt that is a floor, **×2+**, or **×?** when unknown. Each stage shows its total time (**Build 1h**).

- A ring marks where the work is, with this visit's time inside (**now 8m**) when the stage had others.
- A finished work's last stage has a bold name.
- A dashed stage did not happen: its mode has none, or the work went past it. A faint one is not reached.
- A hatched stage may have had a doubt's time; its name sits on a plate.
- A doubt's mark, **▨ 1h 20m**, sits between its two stages: their time together. Both are ringed while the work is in it.
- A colour names a stage, the same on [the trail](#the-trail).

On a narrow card a stage shows its first letter. Before the mode is chosen, all six are faint. Clicking a stage that happened, or a doubt's mark, opens its events.

### Which stage the work is in

The stage is who holds the work. The Conductor hands it to an agent by starting it, or by speaking to it while it is not at work. An agent lets go when it stops, exits, sleeps or leaves; asking you a question is not letting go. The holder is the agent handed the work last among those still holding it, or the last one handed it.

| The work is | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| merged | **Merge** |
| waiting on CI, whoever holds it | **Checks** |
| a green PR handed to you | **Merge** |
| waiting on your answer, before any implementor | **Approve** once a planner has joined, **Plan** before that |
| waiting on your answer, later | the stage it is in |
| with the agents, before any implementor | **Plan**. A plan you answered stays in **Approve** until the implementor starts. |
| held by the planner | **Review** |
| held by the implementor | **Build** |

A draft PR decides no stage. It only cannot be merged.

Which stages a work has comes from its mode:

| Mode | Its stages |
| --- | --- |
| Full | All six. |
| Trivial | **Build**, **Checks** and **Merge**. Nobody plans, approves or reviews, so those stages are dashed. |
| Watch | None, and no map. |

- In a full work, answering the setup form counts as Plan time. A trivial work has no Plan, so its setup is in no stage; that time still counts as **you** in the time line.
- On a work saved before kolu kept hand-offs, time that turns on who held it is a doubt, **Build or Review** or **Plan or Approve**, until the next hand-off. With no planner, as in a trivial work, it is **Build**.

## What every card offers

Under a card's stage map, and at the top of an open row, a card offers **Open PR #N ↗**, to the pull request on its forge, while a green PR waits for you to merge it. There is no merge button: you merge on the forge, or tell the Conductor to.

Picking a work points the chat at it. A work chip in the conversation, which names a work rather than picks it, also offers **About this work**.

## Reach a work from anywhere

Every work has an address, `#/work/…`. Opening it enters the Conductor, picks that work and scrolls to it, or to one question of it. A link to a work kolu no longer holds says so. [Link to any view](/power-features#link-to-any-view) lists the address forms.

The command palette lists what needs you first: a **Needs you** row says what is wanted and then names its work, and a **Works** row gives the title and where the work stands. Both keep their own headers while you search. The **◆** mark on a [dock row](/dock#a-row-in-a-piece-of-work) is a link to that terminal's work.

## The picked work

Click a work, a card or a row, or its entry in the sidebar, and it is picked: it fills the works' side. The chat narrows to the picked work. Click **← All work**, or the work's head again, to go back.

A work that needs you and a finished work can be picked too. On a phone it is a page of its own with **← All work** above it. A reload stays on it.

A switch in the head of a picked work in progress has three views. It opens on **Terminals** while a terminal of the work is up, and on **Details** otherwise. **Code** is in front only when you choose it. A finished work shows only its details.

- **Terminals**: each terminal of the work live, in a pane with the [Look](/tiles#look-from-the-conductor) window's head and **Inspect**, which opens Details on that terminal. One or two stack; three or more, or a window under 1280px wide, show one pane with a tab each. How big each terminal is depends on the pane ([below](#a-terminals-size-in-its-pane)).
- **Code**: the work's own folder in the [Code tab](/code-tab)'s view, read on the work's machine: the tree, the **Local** and **Branch** changes, and the files. It stays after the work's terminals have ended. When there is room, the tree stands beside the file, at a width you drag and this device keeps. Which of **All files**, **Local** and **Branch** you were on, and the file open in each, are kept for the work, across a reload and in another browser.
- **Details**: the terminals' rows and the parts below. Clicking a stage on the map shows Details.

Code shows less in two cases:

| Case | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| kolu has not read the work's folder yet | One line saying so, the same as the Worktree part. |
| No terminal of the work stands in its folder | The tree, the changes and the files still show. Comments are off, and a picture or a download says in one line why it cannot be fetched. |

### A terminal's size in its pane

- On a desktop, a pane sizes its terminal to fill it, never below 80×24 on either side, when it comes on screen and whenever its box changes.
- On a phone or a tablet, a pane leaves the terminal's size alone.
- When the terminal's size has stayed other than its pane's, the size in the pane's head reads **Resize to fit**. Click it once to give the terminal the pane's size, never below 80×24, on a phone as on a desktop.
- **Fit width** (one choice for every pane) shrinks a terminal wider than its pane to fit, and is offered only while it is wider. In a pane narrower than 80 columns it also sets how many rows the terminal gets.
- **Look** never changes a terminal's size.

### What Details hold

Click a terminal's role to unfold its row. A terminal that is up shows the Right panel's own parts for it: the agent card, **Compose** to send it text, and the program in front. Compose writes to that terminal on its own machine, through the same [lock](/tiles#a-terminal-the-conductor-is-driving) as your keys.

Below the rows:

| Part | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Worktree | Once for a work in progress: its branch and repository, the PR and checks, odu's CI, and the folder, as the [Right panel](/right-panel) shows them. It stays after the terminals end. |
| Files | The worktree's `.waterfall/` files, at any depth, and the copies kolu kept. A folder is one chip with its file count, such as `mockup/ · 49`. Click it to unfold it. Click a file to open it in [the reader](/read-a-plan). |
| Pull request | Its number as a link: open, merged or closed, and **draft** while it is one. The last check result, failed ones in one line (`7 failing: ci::nix, ci::unit, +5`) to click. While open, whether GitHub would merge it. |
| Setup | One line: the mode, each role's harness and model, and what happens on green. |
| Decisions | The latest three, one line each. **all N** shows the rest. Click a row for the question. |
| Trail | Every visit the work made to a stage, in order ([below](#the-trail)). |
| Timeline | The events of one stage or one visit, with their time and duration. |

The timeline shows the stage the work is in, or a finished work's last stage, until you click a stage, a doubt or a visit. **whole timeline · N events** unfolds it all in place. A watch, which has no stages, shows its latest five events.

A line runs through the event dots. The gap between two events grows with the wait, up to about four rows, and a wait near that maximum prints its time on the line, such as **3h**.

A question reads **Asked:** and your answer **You:**. A run of files one agent wrote into a folder is one row, such as **Wrote mockup/ · 49 files**. A line too long for the row is cut, and a folder's row lists its files, with **more** beside it.

### The trail

Each visit is one segment above the timeline, as wide as it was long, in its stage's colour. Clicking one, however short, shows its events.

- The visit the work is in is ringed and labelled **now**.
- A run of review rounds is labelled once, as in **6 review rounds · 1h 10m**; every other visit by itself, as in **Checks 12m**.
- A doubt is hatched, set apart and labelled with both stages: **Build or Review 1h 20m**.

### What a timeline keeps

A timeline is kept short in four ways:

- A work keeps its latest **30** agent state changes; older ones fold into one **N earlier agent state changes** line. Hand-offs, and stops that ended a hold, are never folded.
- While a PR's checks run, only GitHub's latest answer about merging it is kept.
- One commit keeps at most **8** check results and **8** merge answers. Past that, an old one in the middle is dropped, never the first or the newest.
- kolu lists the first **200** files and folders in `.waterfall/`, and draws a run of files written into one folder as one row.

Nothing else is dropped, and kolu keeps the **last 20** finished works with their files.

## How a work ends

A work ends three ways: the forge merges or closes its pull request, you click **Stop** on the card and confirm, or the Conductor stops it. Declining the setup of a work nothing has happened in removes it.

An ended card's line says how it ended, such as **Merged #181 · 1d ago**. Open, it shows where the time went, the kept files, the decisions and the timeline, where the merge and the end it caused are one row. It never shows terminals, a lock or a question, and it offers **Reopen**.

kolu copies the work's `.waterfall/` files when it ends, up to 50 MiB per work. Under **Files (kept)**, a line starting **Not kept:** names what was left out and why: a file past that size, a listing that stopped at 200 files and folders, or a copy that failed.
