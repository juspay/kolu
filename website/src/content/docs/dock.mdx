The **dock** is the list of your live terminals, down the left edge of the
canvas. The canvas is spatial; the dock is one ordered list, so a glance answers
what you have open and which terminal needs you.

## Rail and cards

The dock has two densities. **Rail** is a narrow strip of one chip per terminal.
**Cards**, the default, groups rows by repo under a header that stays pinned
while you scroll that repo's rows. Toggle with <kbd>Cmd</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> +
<kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd>; the rows and their state survive the switch.

In **maximized** mode, drag the dock's right edge to resize it, or double-click
the edge to reset to the default width. The width is remembered per device.

## What a row shows

A cards row is two lines. The first reads `indicator · branch · pips · time`.
The second says what that terminal is doing right now:

- the agent's own summary of its work;
- or its phase when it gives no summary: `Starting` then `Ready` before you
  prompt it, `Working` from your first Enter until it writes anything, then
  `Running tools` or `Awaiting input`;
- or the foreground command in a plain shell.

That line ends with the **model** the agent runs on, when it reports one.
Clicking a row centres its tile on the canvas.

## What the indicator shows

The first-column **status indicator** says who is driving and what they are
doing. What each state means for you is on [Notifications &amp;
Attention](/notifications).

| Part | What you see | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shape | an agent's brand mark (Claude, Codex, OpenCode, Grok), or **`#`** | who is driving. The mark appears when the agent's program starts, before you prompt it. `#` is a plain shell. |
| Colour | orange | in flight: thinking, tools, background work, or a shell with live output such as `btop`. |
| Colour | full violet | blocked on you. |
| Colour | dimmed violet | the brief lull just after a turn. |
| Colour | moonlit, row dimmed | asleep. |
| Colour | none, no badge, no wash | [starting or ready](/agent-detection), asking nothing of you yet. |
| Motion | the glyph spins, glows, or holds still | active, blocked on you, or quiet. Reduced motion freezes it. |
| Row wash | violet, with a chip in place of the timestamp | an agent is blocked on you, and the chip says how long it has waited, such as `14h`. |
| Row wash | amber, with a badge on the pip | a [background](/notifications#the-three-counts) ping you have not opened. It lifts when you open that terminal, look at it from the Conductor, or when its agent works again. |

Blocked beats unopened when both apply. An active row hides the "Xs ago"
recency, which would only say "just now".

## A row in a piece of work

When [the Conductor](/conductor#what-it-is-working-on) has a piece of work in
flight, every member terminal's row keeps its name first and carries a small
**◆** mark beside it with that terminal's role: planner, implementor, or other.
Hover the mark for the work's title, and click it to open [that work's
card](/work-card) in the Conductor. The mark comes from the work's own
membership, so the dock row and the work's card agree, and a terminal that
leaves the work loses it.

The mark also says who is typing. A terminal the Conductor drives wears it in
the Conductor's hue, and one you have [taken
over](/tiles#a-terminal-the-conductor-is-driving) wears it in the needs-you
violet.

## The counts in each repo header

Every repo header carries the same three attention counts as the host switcher:
**active**, **needs you** and **unread**. The two you can act on are pills, and
clicking one jumps to the next terminal it counts. The plain number beside the
repo name is how many terminals that repo holds. A host tab shows the active
count as a stripe.

The counts include rows a filter is hiding, so an agent that has fallen out of
the activity window still reaches you.

## Row order

Rows are ordered by structure: repo, then branch, then the order you created
them. A terminal stays where you last saw it until you close it, and a new one
appends at the bottom. Nothing moves because an agent finished a turn, so
<kbd>Cmd</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>1..9</kbd>, which walks this list, keeps naming the
same rows.

A row still shows how long ago it moved, and falls out of the dock when it ages
past the activity window. For a list ordered by what is warm, use the
[switcher](/switcher)'s Recent band.

## Splits

A split is an entry directly beneath its parent row, stepped in one notch and
opened by a `└`. Its parent may itself be a split, in which case the entry sits
one notch further in. The entry is labelled with its working directory.

Clicking an entry lands in that pane. Both the parent row and the entry stay
highlighted: the parent names the active tile, the entry names where the
keyboard is.

## Rearrange the dock

The default order is structural, and you can override it by dragging:

- drag a repo card by its header to move the card;
- drag any row of a branch cluster to move the whole cluster. Rows inside a
  cluster never re-order, and a cluster can never land in another repo;
- drag a row by the grip at its right edge, which appears when you hover, onto
  another row: the terminal becomes a split of that one. Its own splits ride
  along, and nesting is allowed as deep as you like;
- drag that grip onto the repo card's header: a split is handed back its own
  tile, placed on the canvas like any new one;
- a sleeping terminal has no grip, and nothing picks its row up: a press
  anywhere on it starts no drag of its own, though the row still travels with
  its cluster. Only a live terminal can be re-homed, and only inside its own
  repo card.

A drop that makes no sense refuses, and says so while you hover: the row itself,
one of its descendants, the row it already hangs under, or the header of a
terminal that is already a tile.

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> while dragging to cancel the drag. Nothing moves.

Your arrangement is an overlay. A new repo or branch still appends at the
bottom, the filters only hide rows, and <kbd>Cmd</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>1..9</kbd>
names stay stable. A drag writes the full order of everything visible when you
let go. It survives a reload and is kept per host.

A name that no longer exists, such as a closed repo or a branch checked out
elsewhere, drops out while its slot stays put. Re-open the name and it lands
back in its old slot.

## The Needs-you strip

When an agent is blocked on your input, it appears in a **Needs you** strip
pinned to the top of the dock, showing how long it has been waiting. The row
itself stays in its section and keeps its number, and an empty strip renders
nothing.

Only an agent genuinely awaiting you earns the strip; one that has just finished
does not. Clicking an entry jumps to that terminal, or to the tile that holds it
when the entry is a split. Nothing dismisses the strip except the agent no
longer needing you.

Like the section counts, the strip reaches past the filters: a row aged out of
the activity window, or one you have slept, still appears here.

## The Filters row

The dock's footer carries a **Filters** row with two chips over the same list:

- an **activity window** (`All / 4h / 12h / 24h / 48h`) hides rows you have not
  touched inside the cutoff. It defaults to **All**, and the choice persists per
  device. A hidden terminal stops counting toward the app badge, but a fresh
  agent transition surfaces it again;
- a **☾ sleeping** chip hides terminals you put to [sleep](/sessions). The
  window filters by staleness, the ☾ chip by dormancy, and the window also hides
  sleeping tiles, timed from when you slept them.

A single **`N hidden · show all`** reports what both filters hide, and clears
them in one click.
